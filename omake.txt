  This file is the bonus booklet. It explains what is going on, who is
  hitting whom, and why the town of Drohiczyn (Дрогичин) smells the way it
  smells. Nothing here is read by the game -- it is here for the reader.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
0. THE SMELL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  It began on an ordinary day, with an ordinary breath of air.

  One of the three -- AKOB, SUGAR or QAW, depending on who you picked -- was
  minding their own business when the wind changed and brought in something
  that was not wind. It was thick. It was warm. It had opinions.

  It stank.

  Not the honest stink of a garbage day, not the stink of a neighbour frying
  fish. This one had structure. It arrived in waves, in a rhythm, like
  someone very far away was breathing out on purpose and the whole district
  was downwind of it.

  Our protagonist did what any reasonable person does when reality starts
  smelling of intent: they went outside to find the source and hit it.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. STAGE 1 -- THE COURTYARD  (boss: Nikita Likhanov)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  They did not have to look far. Something was already sprinting past the
  houses, holding a fork above its head like a relay torch and screaming.

  That was NIKITOS.

      NIKITA LIKHANOV  /  Никита Лиханов
      ~ ForkLover2000 ~

  Nikitos does not have a plan, a job, or a reason. Nikitos has a fork and a
  stomach, and the two are in constant negotiation. He had been eating
  downwind of the smell all morning, which is the local equivalent of
  drinking from a puddle, and the result was that he had started belching
  and vomiting in patterns -- ordered, colourful, geometric patterns that
  hung in the air and had to be dodged.

      "Ты кто такой? Давай, до свидания."
      ("And you are? Alright then, goodbye.")

      "Никита, хватит рыгать."
      ("Nikita, stop burping.")

  He was not the source. He was a symptom -- the first creature the smell
  had gotten into deeply enough to turn into a weapon. He was also in the
  way, loud, and holding a fork, so he got beaten up on general principle.

  Flattened, deflated and slightly cleaner, Nikitos gave up the one piece
  of information he had: it comes from the west. From Drohiczyn. From the
  castle. And it comes on a schedule.

  A schedule means somebody is pushing it.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. STAGE 2 -- THE ROAD AND THE TOILETS  (boss: Nikita Bukin)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  The road west is where the smell stops being weather and starts being
  infrastructure.

  Two public toilets stand by the roadside, and they are no longer public
  and no longer toilets: they have been plumbed into something bigger, and
  they cough up rainbow-coloured pressure whenever the castle exhales.
  Guarding them, sitting on a pizza he arrived on, is the second Nikita.

      NIKITA BUKIN  /  Никита Букин
      ~ the second-hand appetite ~

  Bukin is what happens when the smell finds someone who was *already*
  hungry. Where Nikitos was a symptom, Bukin is a customer. He has made his
  peace with the new order of things: the castle feeds him, and in exchange
  he keeps the road busy. Watermelons, pizza, spirals, two toilets firing
  in stereo -- Bukin's entire arsenal is food that has been through
  somebody.

      "Ну что, доигрался? Сейчас будет большая пицца."
      ("Had your fun? Here comes the big pizza.")

      "Никита, ты серьёзно приехал на пицце?"
      ("Nikita, you seriously showed up riding a pizza?")

      "Голодный -- значит злой. Лови заказ!"
      ("Hungry means angry. Catch your order!")

  He loses. He does not mind losing very much; the road behind him is now
  open, and Bukin's honest opinion is that the protagonist should go see
  the castle for themselves. He is not being kind. He just wants to watch.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. STAGE 3 -- DROHICZYN, THE CAPITAL OF FART
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  You see it from above before you walk into it.

  Drohiczyn used to be a small quiet town with a bus timetable nobody
  trusted. Now the air over it is *layered*, like water in a glass that has
  been left standing: brown at the bottom, green in the middle, and at the
  very top a thin shimmering skin where the fumes meet clean sky and refuse
  to mix. Nothing in the town rots any more, because rot needs bacteria and
  nothing biological has survived down there for years. The streets are
  perfectly preserved and perfectly uninhabitable.

  The locals did not leave. They adapted -- they hold their breath in shifts
  and consider this normal, the way people in other towns consider fog
  normal. When the castle exhales, the streetlights flicker brown and
  everybody stands still until it passes.

  At the centre, where the town hall used to be, stands the FARTING CASTLE:
  a fat, ridged, pulsing thing that inflates and deflates on a slow cycle.
  It is not decorated with towers. Those are valves.

  Bukin is waiting one last time at the gate, riding his pizza in a circle
  that never opens, and he cannot be killed -- only outlasted. Survive him
  and the gate is yours.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. FINAL -- THE FARTING CASTLE  (boss: Dmitry)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Inside, at the top of the valve, sits the man himself, working.

      DMITRY  /  Дмитрий
      ~ God of Fartilization ~

  Push. Hold. Release.

  He is not fighting anyone. He is on a shift. Every few seconds he pushes
  out another batch, the castle ribs flex, the valves open, and a fresh
  front of stink rolls east over the road, over the toilets, over the
  courtyard, and into the lungs of someone who was having a normal day.
  That is the schedule Nikitos mentioned. That is the smell that started
  all of this. It has a source, and the source has a chair.

  Dmitry looks up, mildly annoyed, the way a man is annoyed when someone
  walks into his workplace during working hours.

  Then he stands up.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. HOW DMITRY GOT THE MAGIC OF FART
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  The version Dmitry tells is short: "I earned it."
  The version the town tells is longer.

  Dmitry was, before all of this, an unremarkable man from Drohiczyn with
  one remarkable habit: he ate everything. Not greedily -- indiscriminately.
  Things past their date, things that had never had a date, things that
  other people had already decided against. Mushrooms nobody could name.
  Preserves from a cellar that had been sealed since somebody's grandmother
  died. Fermented things that had gone through fermentation and come out
  the other side into some further stage that has no word for it.

  A stomach that processes all of that becomes, in a small way, a
  laboratory. And laboratories, run long enough without supervision,
  eventually produce something nobody ordered.

  The story agrees on the night: Dmitry ate a jar of something that had
  been quietly working on itself in the dark for thirty years, lay down, and
  at some point after midnight produced a single exhalation that blew the
  window out of its frame, killed every fly in the house, and -- this is
  the part everyone insists on -- did not disperse. It hung in the room
  until morning, holding its shape, waiting to be told what to do.

  That was the moment. Dmitry woke up, looked at the thing he had made,
  and discovered he could *aim* it.

  This is the MAGIC OF FART: not the smell, but the command over it. Under
  Dmitry, gas holds a shape. It sits where it is put. It travels in a
  direction it is given, arrives on time, and takes orders about density.
  What everybody else produces by accident and apologises for, Dmitry
  produces on purpose and does not.

  What he did with it was the least imaginative thing possible: he applied
  it to the town he already lived in.

  He grew the castle first -- fed it, really, since the Farting Castle is
  less built than cultivated, a structure of hardened air that thickens
  every time he exhales into it. From the castle he set the schedule, and
  once the schedule was running, Drohiczyn stopped being a town that
  smelled and became a town that *produces* smell, exports it, and is
  organised around the production. Every street is downwind of the castle
  by design. The whole place is one enormous lung with a man sitting in the
  windpipe.

  He crowned himself the God of Fartilization, which is the sort of title
  a man gives himself when nobody in range still has the breath to argue,
  and he settled in to work.

  Nobody has told him "no" in years. Somebody is about to.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. THE PROTAGONISTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  AKOB    ~ Твёрдая рука ~  ("a steady hand")
      Slow, deliberate, hits like furniture. Came for the smell, stayed
      because somebody had turned it into a business model.

  SUGAR   ~ Сладкая снаружи, ~  ("sweet on the outside,")
      The sentence has a second half and the second half is not sweet.
      Shares AKOB's weapon and none of AKOB's patience.

  QAW     ~ Быстрый и резкий, ~  ("fast and sharp")
      Fastest of the three, worst at stopping. Takes the whole thing
      personally, which turns out to be the correct approach.

  Any of them can make the trip. The route is the same, the smell is the
  same, and the man at the end of it is the same. What differs is how
  loudly they complain on the way.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. ENDINGS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Beat Dmitry on your own steam and you get the GOOD ending: the schedule
  stops, the castle goes slack, and Drohiczyn's air begins -- slowly, over
  a period nobody wants to estimate -- to return to being merely bad.

  Beat him on a deposit (continues) and you get the BAD ending. You still
  won. It is just that your victory has a receipt attached, and the game
  will read it out to you.

  Each of the three has their own pair. Six in total. See the Trophy room.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. AND DEMID?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  The game is called "Dmitry and Demid" and you will notice that Demid does
  not appear anywhere above.

  That is the point of Extra Stage. He is not part of this story. He is
  what is waiting after it.
